IOT  THE RAMP  —  Knee Positioning System™

Six stable leg positions, secured to any OR table in seconds — no assembly, no sterilization.



Six positions of leg flexion — from full extension to hyper-flex — on one device

What it does

A Swiss-made, all-in-one knee positioner that straps to any standard OR table with two Velcro belts. Surgeons shift the leg between six locked positions by hand — no extra components, no reassembly between cases.

Best fit: TKA and general knee arthroscopy procedures needing fast, repeatable, hands-free positioning.

Device design

•  Full knee extension: raised distal footrest suits larger thighs too.

•  Pressure protection: soft rounded pad cushions the Achilles; ergonomic calf support.

•  Hyper-flex position: foot slides into position-1 pocket for stabilised deep flexion.

•  Footrest grip: microstructured surface, firm flat pad, raised edges visible through the drape.

•  Flexible mounting: multiple slots + rubber feet fit any OR table; two belt sizes available.

Clinical & OR benefits

•  Improved ergonomics: elevates the knee in extension, cutting the surgical-field height change during TKA.

•  Hands-free stability: lateral support + hip wedge give secure positioning across all six positions with unobstructed leg access.

•  Faster turnover: no disassembly, cleaning, or sterile consumables between cases.

•  Lower staffing need: one device replaces multiple assistants holding the leg.

•  Less waste: reusable design, no single-use sterile components.
