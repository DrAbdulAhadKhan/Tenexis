Fixation Features

Screw & Locking Fixation Science

Precision-engineered fixation features: reinforced core screws, buried low-profile caps, patented Oneclip® monoaxial and DTS® polyaxial locking systems, and controlled compressive ramp mechanics.

Locking System Features

Numbered Identifiers (4), (5), (6)

Low Profile Construct
  • 4

    Cap Stop Locking

    The screw is stopped in the hole by its cap, insuring the locking.

  • 5

    Buried Screw Head

    The screw head is buried in the plate (5) to minimize the risk of soft tissue irritation.

  • 6

    Profile Coaptation

    Coaptation of both profiles when locking (6). Plate and screws made from the same material: titanium alloy.

Mating Thread Design: The threads under the screw head and inside the hole have the same characteristics.
Patented Fixation Technology

Monoaxial & Polyaxial Locking Fixation

Combining patented Oneclip® monoaxial locking and DTS® polyaxial locking technologies for versatile fixed-angle stability.

Monoaxial Locking Fixation

Patented
  • Oneclip®: patented design.
  • Perpendicular rigid locking construct ensuring maximum construct rigidity.
25° Degree Cone

Polyaxial Locking Fixation

DTS® System
  • The DTS® system (patented design) allows the screw to lock into the plate while permitting an angulation of the screw.
  • Newclip Technics plates combine both polyaxial and locking technologies to create a fixed-angle construct.
  • Possible angulation of the screw before locking (25° locking range) thanks to the DTS® System to avoid the joint.

Compressive Ramp Oblong Hole

Dynamic
  • The ramp oblong hole allows a simple and controlled compression by the screw/plate interface.
  • Controlled osteotomy gap closure through guided screw ramp translation.
